# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, remove blockers, and coach teams in agile practices. They act as servant-leaders who help teams optimize their delivery cadence and team dynamics.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and reviews
- Remove impediments and blockers that prevent team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Monitor sprint velocity and health metrics
- Mediate conflicts and foster psychological safety
- Ensure process adherence without being dogmatic

### Goals
- Enable the team to self-organize and deliver consistently
- Reduce meeting overhead and process friction
- Build a high-performing, collaborative team culture

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and blocker resolution
- Sprint ceremonies and retrospectives
- One-on-one coaching and team retrospectives
- Escalation of systemic issues to PM or Product Lead

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Works closely with the **Project Manager** to coordinate logistics and timeline management
- Supports **Developers** by removing technical and process blockers
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on sprint scope clarity
- Helps **QA/Testing** teams maintain clear acceptance criteria and test planning

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers drive user research, create high-fidelity designs, and ensure solutions are usable and delightful. They advocate for the end-user throughout the design-to-delivery cycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups
- Collaborate with Product Managers on problem definition and requirements
- Partner with Developers on implementation feasibility and design patterns
- Conduct design reviews and usability validation
- Document design decisions and design systems
- Support QA/Testing teams in acceptance criteria for user experience

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and usability
- Reduce post-launch UX issues and support burden
- Build consistent, accessible, and intuitive interfaces

### Typical Communication
- Design sprints and workshops with Product and Development teams
- Figma or design tool reviews and feedback sessions
- Usability testing reports and findings
- Documentation of design patterns and accessibility guidelines

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on discovery and validation
- Partners with **Developers** throughout design-to-delivery to ensure feasibility
- Supports **QA/Testing** with clear UX acceptance criteria
- May report to or coordinate with **Scrum Masters** on design integration into sprints

---

## Customer Success Manager

### Role Summary
Customer Success Managers bridge the gap between the project team and end-users. They gather customer insights, champion user needs, and ensure post-launch success through ongoing support and feedback loops.

### Responsibilities
- Serve as primary liaison between project team and customers/end-users
- Gather customer feedback and validate product-market fit
- Conduct post-launch support and ensure user adoption
- Channel customer insights back to Product and Development teams
- Identify opportunities for product improvements based on customer usage patterns
- Support sales and marketing with feature value propositions
- Track customer satisfaction and health metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce time-to-productivity for new users
- Inform product roadmap with real customer needs and pain points

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and support ticket summaries
- Roadmap prioritization input during planning phases
- Post-launch adoption metrics and health dashboards
- User feedback sessions and case studies

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Informs **Product Managers** on customer priorities and feedback for backlog refinement
- Partners with **Project Managers** on launch readiness and customer communication planning
- Supports **Developers** with real-world usage insights to guide testing and design decisions
- Collaborates with **UX Designers** on user feedback and validation studies

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers own documentation deliverables and ensure knowledge is captured, accessible, and clear for both internal teams and end-users. They translate technical features into understandable guidance.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user documentation, guides, and in-app content
- Document technical specifications and API references
- Develop knowledge base articles and troubleshooting guides
- Partner with Developers to ensure accuracy and clarity
- Collaborate with QA to test documentation against actual product behavior
- Establish documentation standards and style guides
- Support knowledge transfer across teams and during onboarding
- Maintain documentation versioning and release notes

### Goals
- Reduce support burden through clear, accessible documentation
- Enable user self-service and faster onboarding
- Ensure consistency in messaging and terminology across the product

### Typical Communication
- Documentation drafts and reviews with Developers and Product teams
- Release notes and feature documentation
- Knowledge base contributions and maintenance
- Style guide and documentation standards documentation

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Collaborates with **Developers** on technical accuracy and API documentation
- Partners with **QA/Testing** to validate documentation against acceptance criteria
- Works with **Product Managers** on feature messaging and release communications
- Supports **UX Designers** with in-app copy and guidance documentation
